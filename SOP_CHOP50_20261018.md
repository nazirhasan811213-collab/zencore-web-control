# ZenCore Pine 2.4 — TF3 and TF15

Prepared change; deployment and TradingView alert installation are separate steps.

## Entry

The native chart SOLID establishes direction and remains held under the existing lifecycle rules. Native Movement and HEMA ribbon must match it. Of four current confirmations, at least three must pass:

1. Price beyond the original SOLID entry in its direction.
2. WaveTrend in that direction.
3. Native checklist at least 4/5 green.
4. Directional MTF score (TF3: 5/10/15; TF15: 15/30/45).

Forecast is informational only, including when unavailable. It contributes no confirmation and is not required for data readiness.

The mandatory final gate is the native Pro Fusion Core Intelligence Chop Index from the same Pine snapshot: a finite numeric value in [0,50). Exactly 50, higher values and missing/invalid data reject both BUY and SELL. It applies to initial qualification, execution retries, re-entry and the server's final queued-order delivery validation. The four-confirmation score never overrides it.

## Risk plan

TF3 SL distance = max(frozen ATR14 at SOP completion, 3.00 gold price units). BUY subtracts this distance; SELL adds it. TF15 retains one frozen ATR14 without a price floor.

Only the SL floor changes. Fixed R:R target formulas retain ATR14 multiples 1/2/3, so their actual reward/risk falls below 1/2/3 when the TF3 floor binds. Fibonacci targets retain Pine's existing SL-to-entry ratios 1.618/2.618/4.236 and therefore use the floored SL-to-entry span. No TP cap is introduced.

Entry is the Pine SOP-completion quote, not a guaranteed broker fill. The 3.00 floor is a gold price distance, not a fixed account-currency loss. Existing broker SL execution and step-lock mechanics remain unchanged; no connector or EA files are modified.

## Existing lifecycle and exit

Event lifetime remains 60 seconds from original qualification, with immediate send and bounded retries. A high Chop reading never refreshes this deadline. Re-entry still requires confirmed MT5 closure, a post-close pullback and strong resumption evidence. No same-strategy position stacking is added.

Initial SL closes all. TP1 locks entry +/-1.50 when executable; TP2 locks TP1; TP3 locks TP2; extension strictly beyond TP3 by 3.00 locks TP3. Native Close Separuh and Close All close all. Existing mandatory risk exits remain available regardless of entry Chop filtering, including for positions opened under the previous policy.

## Release coordination

Both new Pine scripts use `PINE_3TRIGGERS_3OF4_CHOP50_V3`; TF3 risk plan uses `TF3_PINE_MIN3_PLAN_V4`. Old scripts cannot authorize new entries after the server policy update. Replace each installed script and recreate its alert because TradingView alerts retain their saved script/input snapshot. The previous policy remains recognized solely where needed to preserve lifecycle and exit handling during migration.

Native TradingView compilation and actual broker fills have not been validated in this environment. Automated tests do not establish profitability.
